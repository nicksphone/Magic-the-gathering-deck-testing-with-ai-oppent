Base: `6c5c62b527208fa881ee9d0df90b85274124e213`. Product and tests were
composed in an isolated source-only checkout, not the live or user database.

## Changes

- Shared complete raw-body validation now runs before cast payment, including
  ordinary permanent casts. Paired kicker, bestow/attached payment, modal counter
  prohibition, entry exchange, turn protection and loyalty bodies retain strict
  unknown-clause rejection. Only specifically matched reminders are normalized.
- Complete typed body diagnostics and runtime admission share helpers rather
  than treating a keyword warning as evidence of execution. Generic source names,
  costs and permitted syntax variants remain supported.
- Raw energy/enchant consumers retain original printed clauses. Ambiguous or
  unknown parenthetical clauses do not gain support through reminder stripping.
  Single-target effect sequences fizzle when that target becomes illegal.
- Complete target-free untap previews use the existing untap effect with no
  manufactured target. Existing selected-target legality and payment still apply.
- Generic land-setting instructions retain explicit granted mana without
  inventing a basic-land subtype; continuous-layer and paid Aura witnesses pass.
- Complete loyalty bodies compile to ordinary effects and retained continuations.
  The shipped printed abilities remain available, including emblem creation,
  delayed untap, permissions, search, token, animation and multi-step effects.
  Only accounted companion clauses act as passive planeswalker abilities.

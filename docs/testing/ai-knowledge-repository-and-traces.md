# Persisted Knowledge In Actual AI Decisions

The Task2.2 consumer has a composed 15-whole-module qualification: 190 ordinary
passes, zero failures/errors/skips, in 401.94 seconds. Twelve owned in-memory
SQLite handles close; the unexpected-denial ledger is empty. This is a
component result, not full hosted CI or measured expert playing strength.

## Data And Decisions

All155 shipped canonical card objects and their authentic ruling lists pass
through `KnowledgeIngestor`, real cache/knowledge tables, profile generation,
new-session reads, hydration, `MatchFactory`, private cold serialization and
public views. Every card retains its private carrier; public serialization
exposes none. Both seats then choose and execute a paid Lightning Bolt after
database disposal. SQL access during the decision is forbidden.

Missing, stale, malformed and conflicting knowledge falls back explicitly.
Actual indexed faces retain their own printed tags. Two different physical
modal cards count as two consultations; repeated proxies for one physical
card do not inflate the count. Proxies deliberately keep their previous lack
of an engine-visible `id`; a separate private identity serves usage only.

## Trace Visibility

`AIDecision.knowledge_usage` contains anonymous per-decision consultation
counts. Existing analytics, head-to-head and overnight producers forward them.
Trace construction accepts only bounded integer counts and the finite fallback
reasons `missing`, `shape`, `face`, and `surface`. Unknown keys, identity-rich
payloads, negative/boolean/nonfinite counts, and inconsistent totals are omitted.
Old decisions with absent metadata are not represented as verified zero usage.
Existing Oracle-resolution counters, action scoring and rule permissions are
unchanged. Full game diagnostic traces remain private.

The actual existing Tempo-versus-Blue-Control CLI runs with its master
difficulty, seed731, one game and original6000-tick bound. The game completes
naturally; the test requires completion and legal outcomes, not a chosen winner.
Each decision exports anonymous usage and at least one uses persisted knowledge.
This smoke is not a matchup-quality comparison or an archetype win-rate claim.

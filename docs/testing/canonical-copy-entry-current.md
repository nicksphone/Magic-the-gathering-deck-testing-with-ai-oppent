## Behavior

Physical printed characteristics are retained independently from a permanent's
copied battlefield form. Existing snapshots without adequate provenance remain
readable, but unavailable copy values are not invented from mutable live fields.
The new record is keyword-only, preserving the old positional constructor and
pattern-matching layout. Private retained records stay out of public views.

Actual copy-entry preparation now carries deliberate select/decline choices
through ordinary casts, supported blink/return routes, genuine copied permanent
spells and the already validated loyalty-return path. A copied-spell candidate
uses one detached native recipe and one reserved identity; it is not prematurely
registered on the battlefield or substituted for the physical source frame.
References, controllers, recipe types, counter and land-entry continuations are
validated before committing. Repeated completion cannot allocate or enter twice.

The AI checks the actual executable choices without filtering the public legal
offer, changing ranking coefficients or treating an invalid retained source as
an optional decline. Human controls display the offered public permanent name
and identity and submit a deliberate selection or optional decline. Missing
public permanents are disabled rather than fabricated.

Copied Auras and other unadapted entry routes remain unsupported; this work does
not claim otherwise. Eligibility alone is not a certificate of executability.

## Limits

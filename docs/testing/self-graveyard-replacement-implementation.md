## Precedence And Limits

Official Comprehensive Rules effective September25,2026 were consulted before
product editing: https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260925.txt.
CR614.15 priority self-replacement is not this static printed self-reference
ability. CR616.1/616.1e permits an affected-player choice among applicable
effects. Thus Rest in Peace versus printed shuffle has no forced self winner.
Trusted explicit replacement-source selections execute either destination;
unknown source or unresolved competing destinations reject without guessing.
Legacy same-destination exile ordering is retained, with no query logging.

The current public additional-cost contract cannot carry the new per-cost
replacement selection. Ambiguous public cost actions therefore reject atomically,
not silently choose; manual competing-payment continuation/UI is unqualified.
No API/schema/helper/engine/mana/cost/parser/frontend/AI redesign is made.

Humility suppresses the battlefield ability, not the same printed ability on
a hand/library card. Leyline's opponent-graveyard condition follows card owner,
not a controlled foreign battlefield's controller. Stale origin, owner or
suppression changes reject a retained plan before mutation. BF/hand/exile/stack
to-library transitions change zone sequence; library-to-same-library does not
invent a zone change (CR400.7/701.24). No all-replacement, token/copy/commander,
batch replacement ordering, unknown ability-loss or global readiness claim.

## Scope

complete noncombat counter-conversion clause recognition, replacement-query

universally authenticated. Heroic matches actual announced target references
and admits the complete supported counter instruction; unrecognized bodies
retain an unsupported diagnostic rather than partial rewards.

## Isolation and Limits

The parent harness denies SQLite and socket operations before pytest imports,
including the tested native/public aliases, and prepends its guard to explicit
serializer-child environments. This is a Python audit/alias guard, not an OS
syscall sandbox or a claim of per-child nonce/sentinel instrumentation. No SQL,
HTTP, browser, LAN, deployment, full-suite or statistical AI qualification is
claimed by this gate. Canonical raw Comprehensive Rules bytes are preserved,
including their existing trailing whitespace.

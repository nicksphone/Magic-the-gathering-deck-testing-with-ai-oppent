# Legacy Selected Mana Guard

Separate validation-only increment over frozen A8c5. Legacy
tap_nonland_for_mana cannot announce an ability index, other-card payment,
hybrid branch, X or mixed BASE vector; reject these requests and require the
indexed typed route. Simple legacy mana and internal executor None/default
planning remain unchanged. No mana source, executor or engine edits.

Both-seat canonical Tower, Prospector, Bog Witch, Cairns and Gyre Engineer
core/HTTP tests cover rejection without root/SQLite mutation and restart;
Simic Growth Chamber covers the actual land-only Sphere replacement.
Sol Ring, Forest and fixed-self-sacrifice Basal Thrull legacy cases remain
accepted; explicit indexed Gyre Engineer and Sphere-replaced Growth Chamber
BASE vectors execute in core/HTTP whole-intent and restart tests. Ambiguous-index
and unsupported-X tests inject negative view metadata on a canonical Sol Ring;
they do not claim a supported canonical X-producing card. Internal resource and
hybrid None paths execute positively; strict AI spell reservation gates use the
parent frozen732 fix, not historical failing source.

Four predecessor Gyre Engineer legacy acceptance tests are intentionally
superseded by explicit mixed-vector announcement at this public boundary;
their source remains untouched. Typed engine vectors and automatic planning
remain supported. Public required_choices intent correction is independent
consumer patch ae1646, not folded into frozen A8c5 or this delta.

This guard covers the confirmed generic tap_nonland_for_mana public bypass,
including land permanents sent through that generic action. Dedicated legacy
tap_land_for_mana and tap_lands_bulk remain restricted to tap-only abilities by
existing validation; this patch does not redesign those routes or claim new
whole-vector announcement support for them. No GUI/full-hand performance or
neural competence qualification is claimed.

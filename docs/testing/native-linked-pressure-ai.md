## Reproduced Decision

The current24 review found an actor-authorised position with two Searing Blazes,
four untapped red sources after a real land play, an opposing5/6 Gearhulk,
actor10/opponent9 and no burn cast. A separate fixed trusted-pose audit confirmed
both funded refusals/both seats. The normal root heuristic rejected face damage;
the required child also rejected a creature that survived one spell. Two manual
paid rules controls independently verified RR4, six damage to the player and
creature, and the creature's death. Those manual controls are not AI success.

The correction recognises the complete linked-damage body, admits safe pressure
after combat, and completes the required legal creature target. It reuses native
target ranking and full payment/Ward checks. The second spell is a new ordinary
decision after actual resolution, not a forced action or a named-card planner.

## Review Corrections

The first draft passed the original10 but failed four genuine review cases:
child reevaluation replaced Gearhulk with a killable retaliating Reckoner, or
paid into Kira's targeting counter. A second draft fixed those cases but still
fell through to the unsafe selector after the ordinary root threshold opened.
An authoritative recognised-child decision fixed that sibling path.

The timing guard was then shown to suppress four normal precombat removal plays:
the unchanged C10 baseline passed them. The final revision puts MAIN2/end-step
and empty-stack restrictions only on new root admission. Normally admitted roots
retain native timing; unknown effect shapes retain the legacy path. All rejected
drafts, harness errors and unmodified tests remain in private evidence.

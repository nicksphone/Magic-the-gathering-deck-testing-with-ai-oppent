# Additional AI payment commitments

The deferred-payment policy counts additional commitments created by effect
sequences and copies, using the copy's controller. Unbound or cyclic referred
copies supply no confirmed count.

An uncertain extra loan remains legal. Learned and heuristic proposals retain an
already proved payment continuation when available, and the chosen alternative
is checked against the authoritative state before returning. The pass
alternative is considered before expensive uncertain branches.

Forecast caching is confined to the current root and the existing complete-action
protocol. An empty cache after a denied forecast supplies no conclusion; an
already proved fallback remains available. Forecast continuations use fresh
states returned by checked admission and native validation for fixed payment and
search choices. An unexpanded financing opportunity does not require retaining
unused frontier snapshots.

The policy changes no scoring weight, strategic depth, hidden-card rule or
payment restriction. Checked current-turn winning routes retain their full
action surface. A profitable additional loan that needs uncertain financing may
be deferred; that preference does not turn uncertainty into a loss proof.

See [deferred-payment forecasting](AI-deferred-payment-20261110.md) and
[simultaneous payment ordering](AI-ordered-payment-20261110.md).

# Complete Seal Support Inputs

Two event-unit tests use Seal of Removal as an ordinary named noncreature
support object. Their constructors previously omitted its printed data. A
complete, unchanged official API response is now pinned in
`backend/tests/fixtures/task13_seal_of_removal/`, with intake provenance recorded
before the fixture changes or tests.

A small local adapter follows the existing fresh-Factory pattern. It verifies
the exact full record, normalizes it, captures printed facts in a private
sample, then supplies only the original ID, owner, controller, graveyard zone
and runtime pose. It does not allocate IDs or advance RNG in the original state.
The two original event bodies and all their assertions are unchanged. Shared
canonical helpers, source manifests, rules and AI policy do not change.

The new native witnesses observe the full Factory input and actual retained
printed facts, original-state preservation and both original scene poses.
They are authored and syntax-checked, not locally executed while the separate
AI measurement holds the native lease. Metadata-expression projections are
not native construction or gameplay passes. Full hosted CI must execute the
new witnesses and complete original module before acceptance.

These are existing manually emitted-event unit seams: they do not supply an
actual battlefield departure, graveyard-entry membership or paid Seal ability
episode. No such lifecycle is invented to make a fixture assertion pass.
The generic observer and other intentional protocol cases stay unchanged;
their specification scope is not waived. This repair is not full-card
activation certification, exhaustive fixture closure, Phase 1 or RC sign-off.

# Siege defeat and transformed casting

Defeat resolves only for the retained battlefield incarnation. Exile occurs
before the permission is bound to the resulting exiled object. The strict
`battle_transformed` permission uses the physical back face, including a
castable modal back under the referenced rules.

Ordinary single-faced copied cards, land backs and departed tokens do not
receive a cast. The permission survives snapshots, supports a durable decline
and rejects forged faces, references, extra grants and wrong actors. A stolen
Battle exiles to its owner while the ability's controller may cast its back.

Normal spell announcement retains taxes, cast events, targets, costs,
restrictions and responses. A suspend boolean is not a transformed-cast grant.
The AI preserves the explicitly permitted face and uses the same admission path.
Finishing the original ability uses the resolving item's kind so it does not
remove the newly cast spell from the stack.

A nonmodal physical back retains the front-derived mana value. A copy of that
back has mana value zero, including a saved spell-copy view after the original
departs. Modal backs and their copies use the face-up cost.

The regression scenarios include permissions, current and restored choices,
countering, taxes, restrictions and copied-back mana value. Shared graveyard
casting controls do not substitute for tests of each physical back's required
targets, additional costs and printed X.

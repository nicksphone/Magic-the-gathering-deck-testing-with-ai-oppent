## Bounded Implementation

Strong may use its existing checked search outside the lethal-trample emergency
when all attackers target the defender, the frame has no pending stack/trigger/
mechanic/replacement choice, and an offered own battlefield Creature has both
validated current major printed advice and positive existing recurring-engine
value. The existing getter checks semantic source/face binding, effective type
and suppression; absent, malformed or stale estimates do not grant admission.
Existing effective permissions, legal intents, checked clones, stack settlement,
combat outcomes and board valuation still determine the chosen assignment.
No printed tier becomes a block permission, guaranteed payoff, or loss penalty.

The existing 4-attacker/5-blocker/4096-node bounds, no-block alternative, budget
exhaustion/unknown fallback, terminal ordering and strong forecast-disabled
behavior are retained. No second projection or combat damage model is added.
The resulting before/after battlefield sets are departure sets, not necessarily
deaths: bounce, exile, reentry and transformations cannot be relabeled as kills.

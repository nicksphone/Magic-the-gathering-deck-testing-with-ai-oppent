# Learned Idle Banking

An opt-in learned decision still validates every admitted Q/V result and terminal
forecast before choosing an action. Generic proactive pass conversion excludes
Foretell and Suspend: their timing, investment horizon and known-answer mana
reservation belong to the existing conservative idle policies.

If the selected action remains a nonwinning pass, those policies inspect copies
of the actor-public view and the **full supplied move list**, Foretell first and
then Suspend. An idle action is accepted only when its exact canonical identity
was already admitted and scored, its family matches the invoked policy, and its
recorded terminal prefix is no lower than the selected pass's. Invalid, mutated,
unadmitted or lower-prefix proposals leave the pass selected. No new action is
materialized or silently delegated to an unrestricted baseline fallback.

Ordinary cast/attack progression is unchanged. Existing helper checks preserve
reserved answers, opponent turns, stack windows, normal-cast precedence and late
or unsafe Suspend investment. Early Control can bank genuinely idle mana without
weakening those checks. Winning passes and late invalid Q/V retain their existing
handling. Unknown forecasts retain the existing prefix semantics; this is not a
certificate of safety against unknown future replies.

Tests use public full-Oracle canonical Rift Bolt/Doomskar positions and explicitly
synthetic finite pass-preferring scores. They establish controlled logic contracts,
not fitted-model strength. No private captures or trained weights are test inputs.
The changed AI source creates a new engine binding: old checkpoints are not retagged
or activated. Default heuristic policy and codec arithmetic are not changed.

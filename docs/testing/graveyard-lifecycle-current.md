## Remaining Limits

Illegal Aura LBF/LKI, explicit human legend keeper selection, other simultaneous
mill/sacrifice/death collection, full stack-face/token lifecycle, current-source
paid optional effects and broader corpus semantics remain separate work.
Warm/cold worker repeats do not multiply unique coverage. A narrow LKI reuse
experiment passed correctness but did not reduce captured chooser latency;
it is not included or claimed as an AI improvement.

Private raw evidence, source archives, complete module list/XML/source manifests
and earlier ledgers are retained on verified NFS under
`parent-integration/graveyard-lifecycle-current-qualified-20261006`.
Active test SQLite stays local; archives are checked before scratch cleanup.
This gate does not certify whole-backend correctness, universal MTG mechanics,
professional AI, a fresh dependency install, operational security or deployment.

# Native Counterspell Tactical Correction

## Evidence And Intent

The unchanged current nine-source/sixteen-class profile passed eight matched
natural deck games, but that is not proof of good individual decisions. A
separate sixteen-case native audit has eight passes and eight strict policy
failures, with no harness errors: six funded, legal Counterspell opportunities
against a lethal Lightning Bolt are declined; both seats miss a legal lower-Bolt
counter before a genuinely paid Vexing Shusher activation resolves. Separate
paid controls demonstrate the lower-target save. They are not AI successes.

## Implementation

1. Freeze all sixteen tests, their exact four predeclared seeds, source/deck
   inputs and raw baseline evidence. Add separate bounded preservation controls
   before final qualification; do not edit the original expectations.
2. Modify only upstream Forge `CounterAi` in an isolated source. Select among
   actual opposing, counterable, legal stack targets, retaining per-candidate
   AITgts and ConditionWouldDestroy checks. Prefer a recognized critical target;
   otherwise keep first-eligible ordering. Preserve mandatory-trigger behavior.
3. A selected-target critical predicate must check observer identity before
   inspecting a public literal, undivided DealDamage leaf. Revalidate its current
   player target with the native fizzle-check API, rather than trusting retained
   target membership after a protection response. Native prevention,
   life-loss and cannot-lose rules remain relevant. Conditional, optional,
   compound, divided, poison/lifelink or unknown amount forms are not silently
   asserted to be lethal. Do not use whole-stack threat membership as proof
   that a different selected target prevents loss.
4. Bypass only discretionary counter preferences for that recognized target.
   Keep targeting, counterability, UnlessCost, affordability and outer cast
   backlash checks. Do not globally reinterpret the ALWAYS profile flags,
   force choices, reseed RNG, or add a simulator/persistent knowledge framework.
5. Extend opt-in profile schema 8 to require exactly ten sources/seventeen
   classes: all prior nine/sixteen unchanged, plus CounterAi. Run new profile
   shape/publication tests red then green; preserve every old test body.
6. Independently compile the new class and qualify the frozen tactical cohort,
   separate preservation controls and matched source-pinned natural games.
   Report divergent histories and incomplete runs honestly; no automatic retry
   or invented win-rate target. Keep private game evidence on verified NFS.

The native replacement forecast is not a blanket hidden-information or query-
purity guarantee. Its limits need explicit review and checks. This component
does not close arbitrary game-winning abilities, general strategy, trained
policy, live adoption, durable saves, BO3, or AI RC acceptance.

## Executed Iterations

The first isolated draft passed all sixteen original tactical cases, but failed
both added Gilded Light internal-protocol cases: its forecast called the retained
Bolt target critical after genuine shroud made that target illegal. Both actual
uncountered Bolts dealt zero damage. That draft and its failed evidence remain
separate; the tests were not relaxed.

The native-target-validation revision passes the unchanged sixteen cases and
both protocol cases. Ten separate preservation controls pass; eight require
exact complete-row equality, including serialized incoming/ending RNG, against
the unchanged baseline. The two actual Oracle/Stifle controls pass too. These
are constructed native cases, not twenty-eight completed games or a global
forecast certificate. A separate Worship protocol then exposed both-seat
false-criticality: native damage prediction returned three, but actual life
reduction stopped at one. That revision remains a held historical result.

The final public-condition revision also passes those two unchanged Worship
cases and four additional nonapplicability controls. Eight separately labelled
synthetic parameter maps reject private/defined/dynamic conditions without
creating a game or invoking those conditions. The packaged twenty-case runner
passes independently; Fog16, Chalice18 and all eight matched natural games pass.
Native forecast completeness, natural-game decision quality, application
adoption and overall AI acceptance remain open. See the final qualification in
`docs/testing/native-counter-tactics.md`.

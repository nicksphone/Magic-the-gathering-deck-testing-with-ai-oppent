# Empty Entry-Only Removal Auras

The attachment projection may defer a known empty linked-removal Aura rather than
crediting its otherwise positive intrinsic enchantment/removal-text value. This is
a narrow local preference, not a claim that every currently empty Aura is bad.

The selected source must have a complete two-line surface: a supported enchant
restriction and one native linked-exile entry instruction targeting an opposing
creature or planeswalker. Extra or unknown text does not qualify. There must be
no opposing effective creature/planeswalker, stack, pending continuation or
unresolved public context. The existing actual paid projection is reused; no
synthetic stack item or extra rollout establishes target emptiness.

The comparison reuses the settled public material summary and checks zone counts,
hand departure and linked records. Only ordinary Aura entry/attachment, paid mana
and funding-land taps are excluded. The native `__attached_to: 0` marker is an
explicit ordinary-attachment operand, not permission to drop arbitrary counters.
Other material, stats, life, resource or pending changes preserve old valuation.

Known cast, enchantment, count, devotion, affinity and applicable blink contexts
also preserve old valuation. A complete self-only removal Aura is not a cross-card
payoff merely because its text says "this Aura"; another such empty Aura may be
held or already attached. Other attachments or animated/resourceful hosts do not
receive that exemption. Native compiled target eligibility distinguishes a real
enchantment blink from a creature-only blink; no generic exile/return word pair
certifies synergy.
Generic trigger wording on a held card is not a live battlefield trigger for the
current Aura cast/entry. Relevant held-card count, devotion, affinity and native
enchantment-blink contexts remain separate preservation checks; a future attack
trigger alone does not establish such a context.
The same zone distinction applies to prowess, extort and cascade keyword facts.
Held storm/affinity facts retain conservative count-dependent abstention. The
canonical held/battlefield Swiftspear controls check actual Oracle and native
battlefield prowess; synthetic keyword-intake controls do not claim new native
payoff outcomes or modify canonical printings.

Human legality is unchanged. Productive entry, other Aura effects, and explicit
targets, payment and hybrid choices retain their existing projection behavior.
The canonical component tests cover both seats, native links, legal empty entry,
multiple self-only Auras, actual blink restrictions and root/input/RNG privacy.
The portable public module includes four two-seat held-Adeline/absent-trigger
controls; all four ran in the 62-pass component gate without private replay inputs.
The subsequent whole-module gate passed all 76 cases, including held-versus-live
prowess and explicitly synthetic keyword-intake controls. The final corrected
40-module cohort passed 966 tests and thirteen subtests. Separately, both original
private recorded positions now reject the empty projection on the final source;
those traces remain private and no learned model was loaded for that check.
Synthetic public-delta controls assert conservative certificate behavior only;
they are not native outcome or strategic-quality evidence. This changes the AI
source contract and does not qualify or retag any existing trained checkpoint.

# Learned End-Step Payment Preference

The learned route shares the existing Control/Counter-heavy/Tempo opponent-end-step
paid-draw economic comparison. It prices the final selected resources, not the
minimum resource in an offered list. The heuristic caller retains its existing
ordering, comparison, and checked-winning exception.

For learned selection, one matching actor-public cost option is retained before
`complete_action` strips display metadata. Missing or ambiguous options and
incomplete/nonfinite prices abstain; they are not zero-cost or infinite-cost
facts. A non-winning paid-draw candidate may yield to another admitted candidate
only when an admitted Pass has an equal-or-better terminal class. All Q values
remain unchanged: heuristic prices are never subtracted from bounded Q.

This is a conditional preference, not a legality rule or strategic-dominance
certificate. Complex selected faces/cast variants, opaque battlefield objects,
stack/choice, delayed-trigger, emblem, and potentially triggered or
replacement-payoff contexts retain the prior learned ordering. Text matching only
causes abstention, never a claimed outcome. Known terminal wins remain first;
explicit costs are not rewritten and no new action is introduced.

Canonical native components plus synthetic Q/terminal-class protocols test these
boundaries. They do not qualify trained weights or playing strength. Changing AI
source changes `_engine_hash`; the previous checkpoint is held, not retagged or
reheadered. Fresh source-bound trained qualification and behavior gates are still
required before activation.

Review R2 extends payoff abstention to trigger clauses after ability-word prefixes
and inside reminder text, plus public effective keywords for the native-supported
Prowess, Training, Undying, Decayed, Exalted, Bushido, Rampage, and Flanking
trigger families. These matches only retain learned ordering; they do not assert
that a trigger will occur, resolve, or be profitable. Canonical full-printing
Magecraft/Prowess controls are separate from synthetic keyword-only protocols.
